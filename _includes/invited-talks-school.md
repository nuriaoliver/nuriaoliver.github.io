Nuria Oliver regularly speaks with students about artificial intelligence,
technology, and careers in science. Her school outreach has included visits to
HHMM Maristas Alicante, IES Miguel Hernandez Alicante, Agora International
School Sant Cugat, IES Joaquima Pla i Farreras Sant Cugat, and IES Maria Blasco.

In 2015, she co-organized TEDxBarcelonaED, the first TEDxBarcelona event devoted
to emerging education, and curated its session on technology and education. That
year she also spoke with secondary students at Montserrat School and presented a
computer science curriculum designed to begin at age five.

Her school and university outreach has included the Leader Code course in
Alicante and talks at the Polytechnic University of Valencia in Alcoi, as well
as a session for primary school students in Muro de Alcoi in 2017. In July
2017, she spoke at the [Science Bootcamp](http://sciencebootcamp2017.umh.es/index.html)
of the Miguel Hernandez University. Earlier, during Science Week at UPC, she
gave several talks to more than 4,000 teenagers, including [“Small Devices for
Big Impact”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p2YcVcDmsp0), in 2013–2014. Her
school outreach also included the [Ingenious! expert session on
AI](http://www.ingenious-science.eu/web/guest/did-you-know-telefonica) in May
2012.
