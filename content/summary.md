I am a computer scientist with over 20 years of research experience in
Artificial Intelligence, Human Computer Interaction and Mobile Computing
at MIT, Microsoft Research, as the first female
Scientific Director at Telefonica R&D and the first Director of
Research in Data Science at Vodafone globally. I am Chief Data Scientist
at DataPop Alliance, Chief Scientific Advisor at the Vodafone Institute
and Fellow at the Spanish Royal Academy of Engineering. I advise
universities, governments and institutions like the European Commission
and the World Economic Forum on Artificial Intelligence and Big Data.
