Over the years, I have served as a conference chair, program committee member,
editor, reviewer, advisor, and academic evaluator across artificial intelligence,
human-computer interaction, ubiquitous computing, and data science.

The page is organized by service area, with program committee service before
2015 available as a separate historical record.

## Advisory committees and expert groups

### Government and public institutions

* **Spanish Government:** High-Level Advisory Board on Spain’s strategic plan for scientific and technological research and innovation (2017–2020), and High-Level Expert Group on a white paper for Artificial Intelligence and Big Data (2017–2018).
* **Valencian Government:** High-Level Advisory Board to the Valencian President on COVID-19 (2020–2022), and Commissioner for the Valencian AI Strategy and Data Science against COVID-19 (2020–2022), leading a 20+ expert volunteer team.
* **Catalan Government:** Advisory Board to the Observatory of Ethics in AI (2020–present).
* **World Economic Forum:** Global Future Council on New Metrics (2018–2019).
* **French Government:** Member of the AI Research and Teaching Chairs program (2019).
* **European Commission:** High-Level Expert Group on Business-to-Government Data Sharing (2018–2020); reserve member of the High-Level Expert Group on Artificial Intelligence (2018–2019).
* **Belgian Government:** External Advisory Committee on the ethical use of mobile data (2020).
* **European Central Bank:** Spanish representative on the euro banknote Theme Advisory Group (2021–2022).
* **Canadian Government:** Member of CIFAR’s International Scientific Advisory Committee for the Pan-Canadian AI Strategy (2022–present).

## Oversight, editorial, and academic service

[ACM IUI

recommendations to the SIGART and SIGCHI leadership on how to best evolve the

### Reviewing, teaching, and academic evaluation

I have reviewed the MIT TR35 Young Innovator Awards (Spain and Latin America
editions) since 2015.

I am a reviewer of ACM Future of Computing Academy 2017, ACM KDD PhD
dissertation award (2017, 2018), French National Research Agenda on AI (2019),
The Web Conference Best Paper Award (2022).

I regularly review submissions for leading conferences, including CVPR, CHI,
IJCAI, AAAI, IUI, MobileHCI, Interaccion, NeurIPS, ICML, ISWC, ICMI,
Pervasive, UbiComp, and PervasiveHealth, as well as for journals including IEEE
TPAMI, IEEE TMM, IEEE TCSVT, and Pervasive and Mobile Computing.

I teach in the
[Artificial Intelligence course](http://www.unir.net/ingenieria/inteligencia-artificial/549201510838/page/2/#-ponentes) organized by the International University of
La Rioja (UNIR).

I have served in the PhD thesis committee of the thesis of Jose Luis
Landabaso (UPC'08), Pere Obrador (UPC'11), Juan Pablo Carrascal (UPF'15), Ruth
Garcia Gavilanes (UPF'15), Janette Lehman (UPF'15), Albert Aminen (ESADE'17),
Taras Agryzkov (Univ Alicante'18), Marie Al-Ghossein (ParisTech'19) and Aurelia
Busto (UA'19), among others.

I have served in the Full Professor evaluation committee of Prof. Oscar
Camara (UPF'19) and Davinia Hernandez (UPF'19).
