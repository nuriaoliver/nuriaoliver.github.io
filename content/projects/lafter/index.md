*Nuria Oliver, Francois Berard and Alex Pentland — MIT Media Lab*

## Abstract

LAFTER is a real-time system for finding and tracking a human face and mouth, and recognizing mouth shapes that correlate with internal states such as happiness or sadness. It has been tested on hundreds of users and demonstrated extremely reliable and accurate performance.
