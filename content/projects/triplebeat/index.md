TripleBeat is the next-generation [MPTrain](/projects/mptrain/) system — a mobile phone-based personal trainer that leverages the influence of music and competition on exercise performance to help users achieve their fitness goals.

## Features

1. **Training proposals:** Based on the user's high-level goals (e.g., fat loss, cardiovascular improvement), TripleBeat selects the appropriate training zone.
2. **Competition:** Users can virtually compete against their past performances or with partners, following the proposed workout as closely as possible.
3. **Audio feedback:** Songs are selected from the smartphone's library based on musical features that encourage the user to speed up, slow down, or maintain pace.
4. **Visual feedback:** A green screen indicates the user's heart rate is within the target training zone; red indicates adjustment is needed. Additional data displayed includes calories burned, workout duration, current song, and competition standing.

## Hardware

TripleBeat uses the [AliveTec](http://www.alivetec.com/products.htm) chestband (with ECG, accelerometer, and Bluetooth sensors) paired with a smartphone.
